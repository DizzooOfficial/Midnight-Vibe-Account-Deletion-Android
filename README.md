## Request Account Deletion

Midnight Vibe: Stranger Talk users can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion:

1. Send an email to **support@dizzoo.com** from your registered email address.
2. Use the subject: **Midnight Vibe – Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include your registered email address and, if available, your Midnight Vibe User ID.
4. Our support team will verify account ownership and process your deletion request.

You may also request deletion after uninstalling the app.

## What Data Will Be Deleted?

Following verification, we will delete personal data associated with your account, subject to applicable legal and security requirements. This may include:

- Account and profile information
- User preferences and mood settings
- Call history and matchmaking records
- Coins balance and premium account information
- Other personal information associated with your account

## Data We May Retain

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, payment records, security investigations or dispute resolution.

Any retained information will be protected and used only for the applicable retention purpose.

## Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests promptly, subject to applicable legal requirements.

If additional verification is required, our support team will contact you.
